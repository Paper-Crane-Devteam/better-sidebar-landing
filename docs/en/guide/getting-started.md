# Getting Started

Better Sidebar is a browser extension that adds powerful organization features to **Google Gemini** and **AI Studio**. It runs entirely in your browser — no cloud, no accounts, full privacy.

## What It Does

- **Folders & Tags** — Organize conversations with nested folders and color-coded tags
- **Full-Text Search** — Find any conversation instantly by searching its content
- **Prompt Library** — Save and reuse your best prompts in one click
- **Google Drive Sync** — Sync settings and prompts across devices (chat content stays local)
- **Multi-Account** — Switch between Google accounts without losing your place
- **Conversation Export** — Export chats in multiple formats
- **Image Downloads** — Download AI-generated images without watermarks
- **UI Customization** — Adjust sidebar width, theme, and layout
