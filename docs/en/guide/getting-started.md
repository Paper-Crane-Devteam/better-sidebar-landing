# Getting Started

Better Sidebar is a browser extension that gives **Google Gemini** and **Google AI Studio** a proper organizational layer — folders, tags, full-text search, a prompt library, and more. It runs entirely in your browser with no external servers. Your data stays yours.

<!-- IMG_PLACEHOLDER: hero-sidebar-overview — A screenshot showing Better Sidebar in action on Gemini, with folders, tags, and the search panel visible -->

## What You Can Do

- **Organize** — Nested folders, color-coded tags, favorites, and pinning
- **Search** — Full-text search across every message in your history
- **Reuse** — Personal prompt library with variables and composition
- **Sync** — Auto-backup settings and prompts to your Google Drive
- **Export** — Download conversations as Markdown, JSON, or plain text

These are just the highlights. Each feature has its own dedicated guide — see [Next Steps](#next-steps) below.

## Quick Start

### 1. Install the extension

Head to the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/better-sidebar-for-google/cjeoaidogoaekodkbhijgljhenknkenj) (or [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/better-sidebar-for-ai-studio)) and install. For detailed steps, see the [Installation guide](/better-sidebar/en/guide/installation).

### 2. Sign in to your Google account

Better Sidebar is **account-bound**. You must be signed in to your Google account on Gemini or AI Studio for the extension to work. The sidebar detects your active account and creates an independent database for it — this is also how [multi-account support](/better-sidebar/en/guide/multi-account/multi-account) works.

<!-- IMG_PLACEHOLDER: account-detected — A screenshot or callout showing the account indicator in the sidebar header -->

### 3. Open Gemini or AI Studio

Navigate to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) or [aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com). The Better Sidebar overlay will appear automatically on the left side of the page.

<!-- IMG_PLACEHOLDER: sidebar-first-load — Screenshot of the sidebar appearing on first visit after installation -->

### 4. Import your chat history

:::tip Important
On first install, Better Sidebar only indexes your **most recent conversations**. Older conversations need their message content imported before they become searchable.
:::

A prompt will appear on first launch guiding you through the import. You can also trigger it manually at any time:

**For AI Studio:**
1. Open the **Search** tab in the sidebar
2. Click the **Upload** icon (📤) in the header
3. Follow the instructions to export from Google Drive and upload the ZIP

**For Gemini:**
Message content is indexed when you open each conversation. There's no bulk import yet — just visiting a conversation is enough to index it.

<!-- IMG_PLACEHOLDER: import-history-prompt — Screenshot of the first-launch import dialog or the manual import button in the Search tab -->

Once imported, all your past conversations become searchable and organizable.

### 5. Try it out

- **Create a folder** — Right-click in the sidebar and choose "New Folder", then drag a conversation into it
- **Search** — Open the Search tab and type any keyword from a past conversation
- **Save a prompt** — Open the Prompt Library and click "+" to save your first reusable prompt
