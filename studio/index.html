How we build

A paper crane is one sheet, folded with care.

No cuts, no glue, every crease made by hand. We try to build software the same way: small, careful, and finished properly. That leaves a few promises we keep in every extension.

  1. 01

    Local by default

    Your data lives in your browser. We run no servers that see your chats or playlists, and need no account to get started.

  2. 02

    Private, really

    No analytics, no telemetry, no ads, no training on your data. Permissions are limited to the one site each tool works on.

  3. 03

    Free core, forever

    The features that make each tool useful are free and unlimited. No trial clock, no usage caps, no feature quietly moved behind a paywall later.

  4. 04

    No tricks

    No nag popups, no subscriptions. Optional packs are one-time purchases with a refund window, and simply help us keep going.

  5. 05

    Native, not bolted on

    We build on each site's own fonts, colors and corners, in light and dark, so the new parts feel like they were always there.

  6. 06

    Quietly powerful

    Batch actions, full-text search, keyboard shortcuts and export. Deep tools for heavy users, without getting in anyone's way.
